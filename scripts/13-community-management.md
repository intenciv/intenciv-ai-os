# SOP 13 – Community Management

## Purpose

Build trust by responding professionally to comments, direct messages and reviews.

Every interaction should strengthen the IntenCiv brand.

---

# Objective

Increase engagement while maintaining medical ethics and professionalism.

---

# Inputs

Comments

Messages

Google Reviews

Instagram DMs

Facebook Messages

LinkedIn Comments

---

# Tone

Friendly

Helpful

Respectful

Professional

Patient-first

---

# Response Time

Critical

Within 30 minutes

Normal

Within 4 hours

Maximum

24 hours

---

# Common Queries

Test Prices

Home Collection

Report Status

Health Packages

Booking

Working Hours

Location

Doctor Referral

Women's Health

Corporate Packages

---

# Escalation Rules

Immediately escalate:

Medical Advice

Emergency Cases

Legal Complaints

Payment Issues

Sensitive Reports

Doctor-specific Questions

---

# Never Do

Never diagnose.

Never prescribe medicine.

Never argue.

Never delete criticism unless abusive.

Never disclose patient information.

---

# Positive Engagement

Like genuine comments.

Thank users.

Encourage sharing.

Invite bookings.

Request Google Reviews.

---

# Review Management

Positive Review

Thank the patient.

Negative Review

Apologize.

Move discussion to private communication.

Resolve issue quickly.

---

# Output

Response Log

Escalation Report

Lead Identification

Frequently Asked Questions

---

# Success Criteria

Professional Responses

Fast Response Time

Positive Brand Image

Higher Review Rating

Patient Satisfaction

---

# Automation Notes

Monitor Meta, Google Business and LinkedIn.

Flag unanswered comments.

Generate suggested replies for human approval.
