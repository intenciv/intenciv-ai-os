# SOP 15 – WhatsApp Campaign Management

## Purpose

Use WhatsApp as a trusted patient communication channel to educate, engage and convert patients into bookings while maintaining medical ethics.

The objective is to build long-term patient relationships instead of sending promotional spam.

---

# Objective

Increase:

• Home Collection Bookings
• Health Package Sales
• Repeat Patients
• Patient Engagement
• Brand Recall
