# SOP 14 – Lead Capture & Conversion

## Purpose

Convert visitors, readers and social media followers into actual patients.

This SOP directly supports the business goal of increasing online bookings and health package sales.

---

# Objective

Capture every interested lead and guide them towards booking a diagnostic service.

---
