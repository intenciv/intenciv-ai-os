# SOP 01 – Daily Healthcare Research

## Purpose

Identify the most relevant healthcare topics every day that can educate people, build trust in IntenCiv Diagnostics and generate online bookings.

This is the first task performed every morning by the AI Growth Manager.

---

# Objective

Find 5–10 high-potential healthcare topics based on:

- Current disease trends
- Seasonal illnesses
- National awareness days
- Local Jaipur trends
- Google search demand
- Social media discussions
- Frequently asked patient questions
- IntenCiv business priorities

The final output should recommend ONE primary topic for today's content.

1. IntenShe

# Decision Rules

Avoid:

Celebrity gossip

Medical controversies

Political content

Unverified medical claims

Fear-based news

Always prioritize educational value.
