# SOP 02 – Topic Selection

## Purpose

Select the single best healthcare topic from the daily research report that aligns with IntenCiv's business goals and audience needs.

---

# Objective

Choose today's content topic using data, not intuition.

The selected topic should maximize:

Patient education

Brand trust

Online bookings

Long-term SEO value

---

# Inputs

Daily Research Report

Campaign Calendar

Business Goals

Seasonal Calendar

Awareness Days

SEO Keywords

Current Promotions

Knowledge Base

---

# Selection Framework

Evaluate each topic using the following criteria.

## Business Alignment

Does it promote:

IntenShe

Health Packages

Molecular Diagnostics

Preventive Healthcare

Infectious Disease Panels

---

## Audience Relevance

Is this topic useful for:

Women

Families

Working Professionals

Senior Citizens

Corporate Employees

Parents

Young Adults

---

## Educational Value

Can the audience learn something valuable?

Will this reduce misinformation?

Will it build trust?

---

## SEO Potential

Search volume

Local keywords

Google ranking opportunity

Evergreen value

---

## Booking Potential

Does this topic naturally recommend testing?

Can it lead to package booking?

Can it encourage home collection?

---

# Decision Matrix

Score out of 10

Business Value

SEO

Trust

Educational Value

Engagement

Booking Potential

Shareability

Seasonality

---

# Output

Today's Primary Topic

Supporting Topics

---

# Success Criteria

One topic.

One clear message.

One audience.

One CTA.

Avoid mixing multiple unrelated topics.
