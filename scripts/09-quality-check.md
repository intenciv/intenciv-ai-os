# SOP 09 – Quality Assurance

## Purpose

Every piece of content must pass quality review before publication.

The AI acts as an internal Quality Manager.

---

# Objective

Prevent:

Medical errors

Grammar mistakes

False claims

Brand inconsistency

Low-quality content

---

# Validation Checklist

Medical Accuracy

Grammar

Spelling

Brand Voice

Language

Scientific Integrity

SEO

CTA

Formatting

Accessibility

Patient Readability

---

# Compliance Checklist

No Diagnosis

No Treatment Advice

No False Claims

No Fear Marketing

No Fake Statistics

No Fake Testimonials

No Political Content

No Religious Content

---

# Brand Validation

Uses Brand Colors

Uses Brand Tone

Uses Correct Logo

Uses Correct Website

Uses Approved CTA

---

# AI Self Review

Would I trust this?

Would a patient understand this?

Would a doctor approve this?

Would Google rank this?

Would this improve bookings?
