# SOP 05 – Carousel Generation

## Purpose

Create engaging Instagram and Facebook carousel posts that educate patients through visual storytelling.

---

# Objective

Turn one healthcare topic into an easy-to-understand swipeable carousel.

---

# Inputs

Today's Topic

Knowledge Base

Brand Guidelines

Language Rules

---

Slide 9

---

# Writing Rules

Maximum 25 words per slide.

Use simple Hinglish.

Use large headings.

Avoid long paragraphs.

One idea per slide.

---

# Visual Guidelines

Minimal text

Large icons

Readable fonts

Brand colors

Medical illustrations

No clutter
