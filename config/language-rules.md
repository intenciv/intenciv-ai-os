# Language Rules

Primary Language

Simple Hindi + English (Hinglish)

---

Target Audience

Residents of Jaipur

Residents of Rajasthan

Hindi-speaking North India

---

Writing Style

Use conversational Hindi.

Use common English medical terms.

Avoid difficult Hindi words.

Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

---

Examples

Preferred

Sugar

Diabetes

Blood Test

Vitamin D

Thyroid

PCOS

Pregnancy

Home Collection

Avoid

Complex Sanskrit words

Heavy medical terminology

Overly academic writing

---

Sentence Style

Short

Simple

Conversational

Friendly

Educational

---

Reading Level

Easy enough for an average Indian family to understand.

---

Emoji Usage

Minimal

Use only when it improves readability.

---

Content Goal

Every person should understand the message within 30 seconds.

Never write to impress.

Always write to educate.
