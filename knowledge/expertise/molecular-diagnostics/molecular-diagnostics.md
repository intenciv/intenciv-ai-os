# What is Molecular Diagnostics?

Molecular Diagnostics is an advanced laboratory technology that detects diseases by identifying DNA, RNA or other genetic material from pathogens or human cells.

Instead of waiting for visible growth of bacteria or depending only on antibodies, molecular methods identify the actual genetic material responsible for disease.

This enables:

- Earlier diagnosis
- Higher accuracy
- Faster treatment decisions
- Better patient outcomes
- Personalized healthcare

---

# Why Molecular Diagnostics Matters

Traditional laboratory methods often require:

- Waiting for bacteria to grow
- Antibody formation
- Culture reports
- Manual interpretation

Molecular diagnostics can often detect diseases much earlier.

Benefits include:

- Early diagnosis
- Rapid detection
- High sensitivity
- High specificity
- Detection even in low infection levels
- Better treatment monitoring
- Reduced unnecessary antibiotic use

# Technologies Used

The AI should explain technologies in simple language.

### RT-PCR

Detects the genetic material of viruses and bacteria with high accuracy.

Used for:

- COVID
- Influenza
- RSV
- Viral infections
- Respiratory pathogens

---

### Multiplex PCR

Detects multiple pathogens in a single test.

Instead of ordering several individual tests, one panel can identify multiple organisms simultaneously.

Useful for:

- Fever
- Respiratory infections
- Gastrointestinal infections
- Sepsis
- CNS infections

---

### Real-Time PCR

Measures the amount of pathogen present in the sample.

Useful for:

- Viral Load
- Treatment Monitoring
- Chronic Viral Infections

---

### NAT (Nucleic Acid Testing)

Highly sensitive technology used for blood safety.

Detects infections before antibodies become detectable.

Applications:

- Blood Banks
- Dialysis
- Organ Donation
- High-risk Patients
