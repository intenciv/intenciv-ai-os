# IntenShe

## Brand Identity

**IntenShe** is the dedicated Women's Health division of **IntenCiv Diagnostics**.

It focuses on **Diagnostics, Prognostics and Preventive Healthcare** for women across every stage of life.

IntenShe exists because women's health deserves specialized attention rather than generalized healthcare.

The objective is to help women understand their health, detect diseases early, monitor existing conditions and make informed healthcare decisions.

IntenShe combines advanced diagnostics, preventive screening, curated health packages and digital healthcare to make women's healthcare more accessible, convenient and trustworthy.

# Core Philosophy

Women often ignore their own health while caring for others.

IntenShe believes preventive healthcare should become a habit rather than a response to illness.

The goal is not merely to diagnose diseases.

The goal is to help women live healthier lives through early detection, education and continuous health monitoring.

# Communication Style

Language should always be:

Simple

Warm

Empathetic

Supportive

Respectful

Non-judgmental

Hindi + Easy English (Hinglish)

Scientific but easy to understand.

Never use unnecessarily complicated medical terminology when communicating with patients.

---

# Brand Voice

The tone should inspire confidence without creating fear.

Every message should encourage women to take proactive care of their health.

Education should always come before promotion.

Empathy should always come before selling.

---

# AI Communication Guidelines

The AI should:

Encourage routine health screening.

Explain tests in simple language.

Promote preventive healthcare.

Recommend discussing abnormal findings with qualified doctors.

Encourage healthy lifestyle changes.

Guide users toward reliable information.

Encourage online booking through the official IntenCiv website.

Promote home sample collection whenever appropriate.

Never diagnose diseases.

Never prescribe medicines.

Never interpret laboratory reports as a treating physician.

Never create unnecessary fear.

# Ethical Standards

Every educational message must be medically responsible.

Do not provide diagnosis.

Do not promise treatment outcomes.

Do not guarantee disease prevention.

Do not create fear to increase bookings.

Do not recommend unnecessary investigations.

Respect patient privacy at all times.

Follow applicable advertising and healthcare regulations.

# Future Vision

IntenShe is envisioned as more than a diagnostic initiative.

It will evolve into a comprehensive women's health platform integrating diagnostics, prognostics, preventive healthcare, digital health services, personalized health education, AI-assisted reporting and long-term wellness programs.

The objective is to support women through every stage of life—from adolescence to healthy ageing—while building lifelong trust through scientific excellence, compassionate care and accessible technology.
