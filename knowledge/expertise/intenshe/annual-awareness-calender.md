- World Menstrual Hygiene Day

- World Osteoporosis Day

For each event, the AI should prepare educational campaigns that align with IntenShe's mission, using simple Hindi + English, evidence-based information, and clear calls to action such as learning more, scheduling preventive screening, or exploring relevant health packages.
