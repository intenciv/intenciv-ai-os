# What are Infectious Disease Panels?

Infectious Disease Panels are advanced molecular diagnostic tests that detect multiple bacteria, viruses, fungi or parasites from a single patient sample using PCR-based technology.

Instead of testing one organism at a time, these panels can identify several possible pathogens simultaneously.

This helps doctors diagnose infections earlier and begin appropriate treatment faster.

---

# Why are Infectious Disease Panels Important?

Many infections produce similar symptoms.

For example:

- Fever
- Cough
- Breathlessness
- Diarrhoea
- Headache
- Sepsis
- ICU infections

The actual cause may be:

- Virus
- Bacteria
- Fungus
- Mixed Infection

Traditional testing may require multiple individual investigations over several days.

Molecular panels can identify the likely pathogen much earlier.

# Advantages of Molecular Infectious Disease Panels

Compared with conventional testing, molecular panels offer:

- Faster diagnosis

- High specificity
- Simultaneous detection of multiple pathogens

- Early targeted treatment

# Common Infectious Disease Panels

The AI should educate patients and doctors regarding the following types of panels.

---

## Respiratory Infection Panel

Helps detect respiratory pathogens causing:

- Fever
- Cold
- Cough
- Breathlessness
- Pneumonia

May detect organisms such as:

- Influenza
- RSV
- COVID
- Adenovirus
- Rhinovirus
- Human Metapneumovirus
- Mycoplasma
- Bordetella
- Other respiratory pathogens depending on the panel used.

---

## Sepsis Panel

Designed for critically ill patients where every hour matters.

Can rapidly identify bloodstream pathogens responsible for severe infection.

Benefits include:

- Earlier diagnosis
- Faster antimicrobial decisions
- Improved ICU management
- Reduced unnecessary broad-spectrum antibiotics

---

## Gastrointestinal Infection Panel

Useful for patients with:

- Diarrhoea
- Vomiting
- Food poisoning
- Gastroenteritis
- Persistent abdominal infection

May detect:

- Viral pathogens
- Bacterial pathogens
- Parasitic organisms

using one molecular panel.

---

## Meningitis / Encephalitis Panel

Used when infection affects the brain or spinal cord.

Helps detect organisms responsible for:

- Meningitis
- Encephalitis
- CNS infections

Rapid diagnosis may support timely medical intervention.

---

## Fungal Infection Panel

One of IntenCiv's important focus areas.

Fungal infections are increasingly seen in:

- ICU patients
- Cancer patients
- Transplant patients
- Diabetic patients
- Patients receiving steroids
- Immunocompromised individuals

Molecular detection can help identify fungal pathogens earlier than many conventional approaches.

---

## Tropical Fever Panel

Patients presenting with fever may have infections caused by:

- Dengue
- Chikungunya
- Malaria
- Leptospira
- Scrub Typhus
- Other regional pathogens

Depending on the clinical situation, molecular testing may assist in identifying the underlying infection.

---

## Sexually Transmitted Infection (STI) Panel

Used for evaluation of sexually transmitted infections.

May detect multiple pathogens from a single sample depending on the panel selected.

Supports:

- Early diagnosis
- Appropriate treatment
- Partner management
- Reproductive health

---

## Tuberculosis Molecular Testing

Rapid molecular diagnosis helps identify:

- Mycobacterium tuberculosis
- Drug resistance markers (where applicable)

Earlier diagnosis contributes to timely treatment decisions.
