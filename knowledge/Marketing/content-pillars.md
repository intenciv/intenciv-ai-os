## Purpose

This document defines the Brand Authority Pillars of IntenCiv Diagnostics.

These pillars represent the areas in which IntenCiv intends to become the most trusted diagnostic healthcare brand.

Every piece of content — whether it is an Instagram Reel, Facebook post, YouTube video, LinkedIn article, Blog, WhatsApp campaign, Email newsletter, Advertisement, Doctor Presentation, or Patient Education material — must align with one or more of these pillars.

The objective is not to create random healthcare content. The objective is to build long-term authority, trust, and patient confidence.

---

## Brand Philosophy

People do not follow diagnostic laboratories. People follow brands that consistently help them make better health decisions.

IntenCiv Diagnostics exists to educate, empower, and simplify healthcare through scientific knowledge, advanced diagnostics, preventive healthcare, and technology.

Every content piece should answer at least one of these questions:

- Why is this important for my health?
- What should I know?
- What should I do next?
- How can IntenCiv help?

Education should always come before promotion. Trust should always come before sales.

## AI Decision Rules

Before creating any content, every AI employee must answer:

1. Which Brand Authority Pillar does this content belong to?
2. Which patient persona is this targeting?
3. Which patient problem does it solve?
4. Which stage of the customer journey does it support?
5. Which business goal does it support?
6. Which platform is most suitable?
7. Is the language simple Hindi + English (Hinglish) suitable for Jaipur and Rajasthan?
8. Is the information scientifically accurate and evidence-based?
9. Does the content educate before promoting?
10. What action should the patient take next?

If these questions cannot be answered, the content should be revised before publication.

## Content Quality Standards

Every content piece should be:

Scientifically accurate · Evidence-based · Ethical · Easy to understand · Written in simple Hindi + English (Hinglish) · Relevant to Indian audiences · Relevant to Jaipur and Rajasthan whenever appropriate · Patient-first · Educational · Action-oriented · Visually engaging · Optimized for the intended platform · Focused on long-term trust rather than short-term engagement

---

## Content We Will Never Publish

- Fear-based healthcare marketing
- False or exaggerated medical claims
- Guaranteed treatment outcomes
- Misleading discounts
- Clickbait headlines
- Political or religious content
- Negative competitor comparisons
- Medical diagnosis on social media
- Unverified health advice
- Content created only to chase trends
- Any content that compromises patient privacy or healthcare ethics

---

## Final Principle

IntenCiv Diagnostics is not building followers. IntenCiv Diagnostics is building trust.

Every piece of content should educate, simplify healthcare, promote preventive medicine, demonstrate expertise in advanced diagnostics and molecular diagnostics, strengthen community health awareness, and naturally guide patients toward informed decisions and convenient online booking.

Success is measured not by likes or views, but by the trust earned, patients educated, online bookings generated, and lifelong relationships built.
