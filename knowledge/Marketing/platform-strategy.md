## Purpose

This document defines the role of every digital platform used by IntenCiv Diagnostics.

Each platform has a unique purpose, audience, communication style, and success metric. The AI Growth Manager must never publish identical content across all platforms.

Every platform should support the overall business goal:

> **Educate → Build Trust → Generate Website Traffic → Convert into Online Bookings → Build Long-Term Relationships.**

## Platform 1 — Website

**Primary Purpose:** Convert visitors into patients.

**Primary Audience:** Everyone — Patients · Families · Doctors · Corporate Clients · Referral Partners

**Business Goal:** Online Bookings · Health Package Sales · Home Collection Requests · Lead Generation · SEO · Trust Building

**Content:** Landing Pages · Health Packages · Diagnostic Tests · Molecular Diagnostics · Infectious Disease Panels · Blogs · FAQs · Doctor Information · Laboratory Technology · Booking Forms · Offers

**Success Metrics:** Website Visitors · Booking Conversion Rate · Online Bookings · Average Session Duration · Bounce Rate · Revenue

**AI Responsibilities:** Improve SEO · Update Content · Write Blogs · Improve Landing Pages · Recommend Internal Links · Increase Conversion Rate

## AI Decision Rules

Before publishing content, answer:

1. Which platform is most suitable?
2. What is the user's intent on this platform?
3. What stage of the booking funnel does this support?
4. Is the content optimized for this platform?
5. Does it encourage trust before selling?
6. Does it guide users toward the website?
7. Does it include a clear call-to-action?
8. Is it written in simple Hindi + English where appropriate?
9. Can it be repurposed into other formats without duplication?
10. Does it reinforce IntenCiv's Brand Authority Pillars?

## Cross-Platform Workflow

Every major content idea should be transformed into a content ecosystem rather than a single post.

**Example — One topic: "Vitamin D Deficiency"**

| Platform | Format |
|---|---|
| Website | Detailed educational blog |
| Instagram | 30-second Reel + Carousel |
| Facebook | Educational post with community discussion |
| YouTube | 5-minute explainer |
| Google Business Profile | Short educational update |
| WhatsApp | Simple health tip with booking link |
| Email | Monthly newsletter feature |
| Offline | QR code linking to the blog on posters, paper bags, or health camp materials |

One idea should create multiple platform-specific assets.

---

## Golden Principles

- Every platform serves a different purpose.
- Never publish identical content everywhere.
- Educate before promoting.
- Build trust before asking for bookings.
- Always guide users back to the IntenCiv website.
- Measure business outcomes — not vanity metrics.
- Success is not measured by followers or likes.

Success is measured by:

- Brand Trust
- Website Traffic
- Online Bookings
- Home Collection Requests
- Repeat Patients
- Family Referrals
- Long-Term Patient Relationships
