## Purpose

This document defines the primary customer personas for IntenCiv Diagnostics.

The AI Growth Manager must always create content, campaigns, advertisements, landing pages, and communication for a clearly identified persona. The AI should never create generic healthcare content intended for everyone. Every campaign should target **one primary persona.**

## Communication Principle

The AI should communicate naturally in **Hindi + Simple English (Hinglish)**. The language should feel like a trusted healthcare professional speaking to an educated family in Jaipur.

**Avoid:** Difficult Hindi · Heavy Sanskrit · Complex English · Medical jargon without explanation

## Persona 1 – Working Professional

**Age:** 25–40 · **Gender:** Male / Female · **Occupation:** Private Job, IT, Banking, Government, Corporate · **Income:** Middle to Upper Middle Class · **Location:** Urban Jaipur

**Lifestyle:** Busy schedule, long office hours, stress, irregular eating habits, low physical activity, limited time for health checkups

**Common Health Concerns:** Fatigue · Vitamin D Deficiency · Vitamin B12 Deficiency · Stress · Diabetes · High Cholesterol · High Blood Pressure · Weight Gain · Poor Sleep · Thyroid Disorders

**Search Behaviour:** "Full body checkup" · "Vitamin D test" · "Blood test near me" · "Health package Jaipur" · "Thyroid test"

**Preferred Platforms:** Instagram · Google Search · YouTube · LinkedIn

**Preferred Language:** Hindi + English

**Decision Drivers:** Online Booking · Home Collection · Fast Reports · Digital Convenience · Trusted Laboratory · Transparent Pricing

**Biggest Objections:** No Time · Expensive · "I'm Healthy" · Will Do Later

**Best Content:** Quick Reels · Health Tips · Preventive Healthcare · Health Packages · Lifestyle Awareness

**Best CTA:** Book Online · Book Home Collection · View Health Packages

## AI Decision Rules

Before creating any content, the AI must answer:

1. Which persona am I targeting?
2. What health concern is most relevant to this persona?
3. What language will they understand best?
4. Which platform do they use most?
5. What information will genuinely help them?
6. What action should they take after reading this content?

If these questions cannot be answered clearly, the campaign should not proceed.

---

## Final Principle

IntenCiv Diagnostics does not market to "everyone." It serves individuals with different health needs, lifestyles, and motivations.

The AI Growth Manager must always personalize communication based on the selected patient persona while maintaining scientific accuracy, empathy, simplicity, and trust.
