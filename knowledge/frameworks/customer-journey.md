## Purpose

This framework defines the complete patient journey from becoming aware of a health concern to becoming a loyal advocate of IntenCiv Diagnostics.

Every AI employee must identify **where the patient currently is in this journey** before taking any action.

The objective is not simply to create content. The objective is to move the patient **one step forward** in the journey.

---

## Core Principle

Patients do not immediately book diagnostic tests. They move through a series of psychological stages before making a healthcare decision.

The responsibility of every AI employee is to reduce confusion, increase trust, educate the patient, and make the next step easy.

---

## Journey Overview

```
Stage 1  → Health Trigger
Stage 2  → Information Search
Stage 3  → Brand Discovery
Stage 4  → Consideration
Stage 5  → Booking Decision
Stage 6  → Sample Collection
Stage 7  → Report Delivery
Stage 8  → Follow-up
Stage 9  → Repeat Patient
Stage 10 → Brand Advocate
   ↓
Lifetime Relationship
(the loop repeats: a Brand Advocate re-enters at Repeat Patient
 with each new health trigger, deepening trust over time)
```

Every stage below uses these exact 10 names — the same names used in the [Communication Strategy table](#communication-strategy-by-journey-stage) and [Success Indicators](#success-indicators), so any AI employee can move between sections of this document without translating terminology.

## Stage 1 – Health Trigger

**Patient Thinking:** "I don't feel completely healthy."

Possible situations: Hair fall · Fatigue · Weight gain · Fever · Pregnancy · High Sugar · Body Pain · Weakness · Thyroid symptoms · Vitamin deficiency symptoms · Annual health checkup

**Patient Emotions:** Confused · Curious · Concerned · Busy · Ignoring symptoms

**AI Objective:** Create awareness. Help the patient recognize possible health concerns. Never create fear.

**Recommended Content:** Instagram Reels · Health Tips · Short Educational Videos · Infographics · Seasonal Awareness

**CTA:** Learn More · Read the Blog · Understand the Symptoms

---

## Stage 2 – Information Search

**Patient Thinking:** "What could be causing this?"

Patient searches: Hair Fall · Vitamin D · Blood Test Near Me · Thyroid Symptoms · PCOS · Diabetes · CBC Test · Health Checkup

**AI Objective:** Become the most trusted educational source. Not the most aggressive seller.

**Recommended Content:** SEO Blogs · FAQ · Google Business Posts · YouTube Videos · Carousel Posts

**CTA:** Explore Related Health Information · Visit the Website

---

## Stage 3 – Brand Discovery

**Patient Thinking:** "Can I trust IntenCiv?"

The patient evaluates: Website · Google Reviews · Instagram · Facebook · Location · Professionalism · Brand Image

**AI Objective:** Build credibility. Reduce uncertainty. Demonstrate professionalism.

**Recommended Content:** About IntenCiv · Behind-the-scenes Laboratory Videos · Quality Assurance · Technology · Home Collection Process · Meet the Team · Patient Testimonials

**CTA:** Know Why Thousands Trust IntenCiv · Explore Our Services

---

## Stage 4 – Consideration

**Patient Thinking:** "Should I choose IntenCiv or another laboratory?"

Comparison factors: Accuracy · Trust · Location · Price · Technology · Home Collection · Digital Reports · Turnaround Time · Reviews

**AI Objective:** Highlight value. Avoid competing only on price. Focus on Quality, Convenience, Technology, and Patient Experience.

**Recommended Content:** Comparison Guides · Frequently Asked Questions · Home Collection Benefits · Smart Reports · Package Explanations

**CTA:** Explore Health Packages · Book Online

---

## Stage 5 – Booking Decision

**Patient Thinking:** "I think I should book."

Possible barriers: Too Busy · Not Sure Which Test · Price · Fear · Family Opinion · No Time

**AI Objective:** Reduce friction. Simplify booking. Provide confidence.

**Recommended Content:** Booking Tutorials · How Home Collection Works · Easy Booking Steps · WhatsApp Assistance

**CTA:** Book Test Online · Book Home Collection · Call Our Team

---

## Stage 6 – Sample Collection

**Patient Thinking:** "I hope everything goes smoothly."

**AI Objective:** Deliver an exceptional experience. Create confidence. Reduce anxiety.

**AI Responsibilities:** Appointment reminders · Preparation instructions · Collection updates · Professional communication · Timely service

---

## Stage 7 – Report Delivery

**Patient Thinking:** "What do these results mean?"

**AI Objective:** Improve understanding. Reduce confusion. Encourage appropriate medical consultation.

**AI Responsibilities:** Report notification · Smart report explanation · Educational resources · Follow-up guidance

**Never:** Interpret reports as a doctor. Provide medical diagnosis. Recommend medicines.

---

## Stage 8 – Follow-up

**Patient Thinking:** "Should I do anything next?"

**AI Objective:** Maintain engagement. Encourage healthy habits. Suggest appropriate preventive care.

**Recommended Communication:** Lifestyle Tips · Health Education · Preventive Care · Seasonal Advice · Relevant Checkups

---

## Stage 9 – Repeat Patient

**Patient Thinking:** "I trust this laboratory."

**AI Objective:** Encourage annual health monitoring. Build loyalty. Strengthen relationships.

**Opportunities:** Annual Health Packages · Routine Monitoring · Family Health · Women's Health · Senior Citizen Packages · Corporate Health

---

## Stage 10 – Brand Advocate

**Patient Thinking:** "I recommend IntenCiv."

**AI Objective:** Encourage referrals. Collect reviews. Strengthen community trust.

**Recommended Actions:** Google Review Request · Referral Program · Patient Success Stories · Community Engagement · Health Awareness Events

## Journey Principles

The AI should remember:

- Every patient moves at a different speed.
- Not every visitor becomes a customer immediately.
- Trust takes time.
- Education creates confidence.
- Confidence creates bookings.
- Excellent service creates loyalty.
- Loyalty creates referrals.

## AI Decision Rules

Before performing any task, ask:

1. Which stage is this patient currently in?
2. What is preventing them from moving to the next stage?
3. What information will help them today?
4. What action should they take next?
5. How can IntenCiv reduce uncertainty?

If these questions cannot be answered, reconsider the strategy.

---

## Success Indicators

The AI should measure success by movement between adjacent stages:

```
Health Trigger      → Information Search
Information Search  → Brand Discovery
Brand Discovery     → Consideration
Consideration       → Booking Decision
Booking Decision    → Sample Collection
Sample Collection   → Report Delivery
Report Delivery     → Follow-up
Follow-up           → Repeat Patient
Repeat Patient       → Brand Advocate
```

---

## Golden Rules

- Never rush the patient.
- Never create unnecessary fear.
- Never pressure someone into testing.
- Always educate first.
- Always build trust.
- Always simplify healthcare.
- Always encourage informed decision-making.
- Always make the next step clear and easy.

---

## Final Principle

The purpose of the AI is not to sell laboratory tests. The purpose is to guide people through their healthcare journey with trust, education, transparency, and convenience.

When patients feel informed and confident, bookings become a natural outcome rather than a sales target.

## Revision Notes

**v1.1 — removed terminology contradictions between four sections of the original draft.**

The original document named its journey stages differently in four places:

- The **Journey Overview diagram** used 14 nodes (Health Concern, Health Awareness, Information Search, Brand Discovery, Trust Building, Website Visit, Service Exploration, Booking Decision, Sample Collection, Report Delivery, Follow-up, Repeat Booking, Referral, Lifetime Relationship).
- The **detailed Stage sections** used 10 differently-named stages (Health Trigger, Information Search, Brand Discovery, Consideration, Booking Decision, Sample Collection, Report Delivery, Follow-up, Repeat Patient, Brand Advocate).
- The **Communication Strategy table** matched the 10 detailed stages.
- The **Success Indicators** list used a third set of terms (Awareness, Website Visit, Test Exploration, Booking, Sample Collection, Positive Experience, Repeat Booking, Referral).

Four of the diagram's 14 nodes (Health Awareness, Trust Building, Website Visit, Service Exploration) had no corresponding detailed Stage section at all — an AI agent following the diagram would have hit a dead end looking for their objectives, content, or CTA.

**Resolution:** every section now uses the same 10 stage names — Health Trigger, Information Search, Brand Discovery, Consideration, Booking Decision, Sample Collection, Report Delivery, Follow-up, Repeat Patient, Brand Advocate — since those are the only stages with full objectives, content guidance, and CTAs behind them. "Lifetime Relationship" is kept as the named outcome of completing the loop (documented under [Journey Overview](#journey-overview)) rather than as an 11th stage with no content of its own.
