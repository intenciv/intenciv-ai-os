## Company Overview

IntenCiv Diagnostics is the diagnostics division of IntenCiv Healthcare Services Pvt. Ltd.

The organization is committed to making high-quality, accurate, technology-driven, and patient-centric diagnostic services accessible to individuals, families, healthcare professionals, and organizations.

The company combines modern laboratory diagnostics, digital healthcare technologies, home sample collection, preventive healthcare programs, and AI-powered patient experiences to improve healthcare accessibility and patient outcomes.

IntenCiv is not just a pathology laboratory. It is building an integrated diagnostic ecosystem focused on **preventive healthcare, precision diagnostics, digital convenience, and patient education** — positioned publicly around **molecular diagnostics**, ID-NAT blood-safety screening, and women-focused prognostics.

## Brand Divisions

The live site organizes the business into **five core divisions**, plus a flagship AI feature layered across them:

| Division | Focus |
|---|---|
| **AI Smart Report** *(flagship feature, not a lab division)* | Plain-language AI interpretation included with every report; standard on all IntenShe packages |
| **01 · ID-NAT** | Individual Donor Nucleic Acid Testing — blood bank safety screening for HIV, HBV & HCV; detects infection before antibodies appear |
| **02 · Infectious Disease** | Panel-based molecular testing for rapid identification of infectious causes |
| **03 · IntenShe** | Women-focused diagnostics & prognostics — fertility, hormonal health, cervical screening, and more across every life stage |
| **04 · Health Packages** | Curated preventive health checkup packages for individuals and families |
| **05 · Bio-Research** | Bio-research support & lab partnerships — biobanking, Active Case Finding (ACF) projects, TB elimination, and public health programmes |

## Language Strategy

**Primary Market:** Jaipur

**Preferred Language:** Hindi + Simple English (Hinglish)

Communication should sound natural to educated families living in Jaipur and North India. Medical terminology that is commonly understood should remain in English — e.g. Blood Test, Vitamin D, Thyroid, Diabetes, PCOS, CBC, HbA1c, Lipid Profile.

Avoid unnecessary translations that reduce clarity.
