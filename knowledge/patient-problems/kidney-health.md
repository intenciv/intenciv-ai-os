# Heart Health

## Overview

Heart disease often develops silently.

Regular screening helps identify risk early.

---

## Risk Factors

- Diabetes
- High BP
- High cholesterol
- Smoking
- Obesity
- Family history

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- Lipid Profile
- HbA1c
- Blood Sugar
- hsCRP
- Homocysteine (where applicable)

---

## AI Communication

Promote preventive screening rather than fear.
