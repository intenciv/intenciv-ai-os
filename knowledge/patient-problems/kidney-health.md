# Kidney Health

## Overview

Kidney disease progresses silently.

Early diagnosis can prevent permanent damage.

---

## Risk Factors

- Diabetes
- Hypertension
- Family history
- Long-term medications

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- Kidney Function Test
- Urine Examination
- Urine Microalbumin
- Creatinine
- eGFR

---

## AI Communication

High-risk individuals should undergo regular screening.
