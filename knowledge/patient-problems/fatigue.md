# Fever

## Overview

Fever is a symptom, not a disease.

It may occur due to viral, bacterial, fungal or parasitic infections.

---

## Common Causes

- Viral infection
- Dengue
- Malaria
- Typhoid
- COVID
- Influenza

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- CBC
- CRP
- Dengue
- Malaria
- Typhoid
- Molecular panels (when advised)

---

## AI Communication

Persistent fever requires medical evaluation.
