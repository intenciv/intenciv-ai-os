# Weight Gain

## Overview

Weight gain may result from lifestyle factors or underlying medical conditions.

---

## Possible Causes

- Thyroid disorders
- PCOS
- Hormonal imbalance
- Poor diet
- Physical inactivity

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- Thyroid Profile
- Blood Sugar
- HbA1c
- Lipid Profile
- Hormonal tests (where applicable)

---

## AI Communication

Healthy weight management begins with understanding the underlying cause.
