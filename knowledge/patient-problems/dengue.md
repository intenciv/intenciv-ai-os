# Dengue

## Overview

Dengue is a mosquito-borne viral infection common during monsoon seasons.

Early diagnosis is important.

---

## Symptoms

- High fever
- Severe body pain
- Headache
- Vomiting
- Skin rash
- Low platelet count

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- Dengue NS1 Antigen
- Dengue IgM
- Dengue IgG
- CBC
- Platelet Count

---

## AI Communication

Never create panic regarding platelet count.

Explain that early testing helps doctors monitor disease progression.
