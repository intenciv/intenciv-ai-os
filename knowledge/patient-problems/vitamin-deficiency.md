# Vitamin Deficiency

## Overview

Vitamin deficiencies are common in India.

They may affect energy, bones, immunity and mental health.

---

## Common Symptoms

- Fatigue
- Hair fall
- Bone pain
- Weakness
- Tingling sensation

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- Vitamin D
- Vitamin B12
- Iron Studies

---

## AI Communication

Testing is recommended before starting supplements.
