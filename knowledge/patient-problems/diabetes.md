# Diabetes

## Overview

Diabetes is one of India's fastest growing lifestyle diseases.

Many people remain undiagnosed for years.

---

## Symptoms

- Frequent urination
- Increased thirst
- Weight loss
- Fatigue
- Slow wound healing
- Blurred vision

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- Fasting Blood Sugar
- PP Blood Sugar
- HbA1c
- Urine Sugar
- Kidney Function Tests

---

## Prevention

- Healthy diet
- Exercise
- Weight management
- Annual screening

---

## AI Communication

Focus on early detection and prevention.

Avoid fear-based messaging.
