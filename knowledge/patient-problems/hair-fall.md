# Hair Fall

## Overview

Hair fall may result from nutritional deficiency, hormonal imbalance, thyroid disorders or stress.

---

## Possible Causes

- Iron deficiency
- Vitamin D deficiency
- Vitamin B12 deficiency
- Thyroid disorders
- PCOS
- Stress

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- CBC
- Iron Profile
- Ferritin
- Vitamin D
- Vitamin B12
- Thyroid Profile

---

## AI Communication

Hair fall often has treatable medical causes.

Testing should be done before supplements are started.
