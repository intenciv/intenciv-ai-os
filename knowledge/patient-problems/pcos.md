# PCOS

## Overview

PCOS is one of the most common hormonal disorders among women.

IntenShe focuses on awareness and early diagnosis.

---

## Symptoms

- Irregular periods
- Acne
- Hair fall
- Weight gain
- Infertility
- Facial hair growth

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- Hormonal Profile
- Thyroid Profile
- Blood Sugar
- Insulin (where advised)
- Ultrasound (by clinician)

---

## AI Communication

PCOS is manageable with early diagnosis and lifestyle changes.
