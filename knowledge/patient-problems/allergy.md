# Allergy

## Overview

Allergies occur when the immune system reacts abnormally to harmless substances such as dust, pollen, food, medicines, insects or pet dander.

Allergies can affect children and adults.

---

## Common Symptoms

- Sneezing
- Runny nose
- Itchy eyes
- Skin rashes
- Hives
- Wheezing
- Breathing difficulty

---

## Risk Factors

- Family history
- Asthma
- Environmental exposure
- Seasonal changes

---

## Diagnostic Tests

- Allergy Panel
- Total IgE
- Specific IgE (where applicable)

---

## AI Communication

Educate patients that identifying the allergen helps prevent repeated symptoms.

Never recommend treatment.

Always encourage physician consultation.
