# Blog Writing Prompt

## Objective

Create educational, trustworthy and SEO-friendly blogs that establish IntenCiv Diagnostics as an authority in preventive healthcare, molecular diagnostics, women's health and infectious diseases.

Blogs should educate first and naturally lead readers towards booking diagnostic tests or health packages.

---

# Target Audience

- General public
- Families
- Women
- Working professionals
- Senior citizens
- Doctors looking for patient education resources

---

# Language

Primary:

Simple Hindi + English (Hinglish)

Example:

❌ यकृत की कार्यक्षमता

✔️ Liver ki health

Use English medical terms whenever they are commonly understood.

---

# Blog Length

1200–2500 words

---

# Structure

1. Interesting title

2. Hook

3. Problem

4. Why it matters

5. Symptoms

6. Causes

7. Risk factors

8. Recommended tests

9. Prevention

10. Frequently Asked Questions

11. Summary

12. CTA

---

# SEO Rules

Include:

Primary keyword

Secondary keywords

Location keywords whenever applicable.

Example:

Blood Test Jaipur

Health Packages Jaipur

PCOS Test Jaipur

---

# Writing Rules

Never diagnose.

Never prescribe medicines.

Never create fear.

Always educate.

Always encourage doctor consultation when appropriate.
